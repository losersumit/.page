UVS Bot Privacy Policy

Understand how the Unified VTC System (UVS) bot collects, stores, and uses your data.

Last Updated: August 6, 2026

Unified VTC System ("we," "our," or "us") operates the UVS Discord bot ("UVS Bot"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details what information we collect, how it is used, and your control options.

1. Information We Collect

The UVS Bot collects and processes limited data to perform its functions as a virtual trucking log, leaderboards, and cross-server transmission tool. This includes:

  • Discord Account Information: Discord User IDs, usernames, and server-specific nicknames (used to create personal profiles, record scores, and map stats).
  • Guild/Server Information: Server IDs, channel IDs, configuration settings, and authorized member lists (used for server white-listing, leaderboard message locations, and permissions).
  • Image Submissions: Job log screenshot attachments submitted by users in designated channels. These screenshots are processed using AI vision endpoints (such as Google Gemini/Groq APIs) to extract trucking data (e.g., speed, level, mileage, clean deliveries) and update database stats.
  • Radio Transmission Content: Messages sent by users in channels explicitly configured for UVS cross-server radio frequencies (+rd prefix/channels). These messages are temporarily processed to distribute them to other connected Discord servers on the same frequency.

2. How We Use the Information

We use the collected information solely to provide and improve UVS features, including:

  • Updating and displaying Virtual Trucking Company (VTC) leaderboards (distance, level, speed, net worth, time, safety stats).
  • Verifying trucking telemetry submissions for anti-cheat protection.
  • Syncing display names and checking server member changes for recruitment/surveillance metrics.
  • Routing cross-server message transmissions on the central radio frequency network.

3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

Your data is securely stored in our central database hosted on Supabase. Additionally, screenshot uploads are analyzed via third-party AI vision endpoints (Google Gemini and Groq API keys) for telemetric data parsing. We do not sell, trade, or distribute your personal data to any external marketing agencies.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain statistics and user data as long as you remain a member of an approved UVS-integrated server or VTC. You have the right to request deletion of your information at any time. Upon request, all stats, logs, and account associations will be permanently removed from our active database.

5. Contact for Data Requests

If you want to request data deletion, review your stored data, or report concerns, please contact the UVS Administration Team via our official Discord Server or email us directly at contact@nmc-logistics.xyz.
